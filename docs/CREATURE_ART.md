# Reviewed creature artwork

The game uses the accepted 105-entry creature library for every enemy form: 93 base enemies and 12 variants. Its 2,577 registered source PNGs are packed without resizing, trimming, recoloring or changing frame order; what the author drew afterwards is packed beside them: the stoop of seven colossi (14 frames) and its in-between pose (7), the nine bosses' left and back attack and hurt (126), 'Umr at-Tawil drawn again whole (his 17 frames put in place of the old, and 4 more for a right view of his own), and Nyarlathotep's two forms drawn again whole (the pharaoh in robes and the Crawling Chaos: 55 frames put in place of the old, and 8 more for the Chaos's longer side walks); round 92 drew Nug, Yeb and Shub-Niggurath again whole, the pack then holding 2,850 frames.

## Runtime files

- `public/creatures/reviewed/index.json`: page rectangles, native canvas sizes, scales, directions, mirror flags and action registrations.
- `public/creatures/reviewed/creatures-0.png` through `creatures-3.png`: four lossless PNG texture pages, with transparent gutters and nearest filtering; `creatures-4.png` holds the fourteen stoop frames, `creatures-5.png` the seven in-between frames and `creatures-6.png` the bosses' side and back frames (126 at first; Yog-Sothoth's ten went in round 56 and Shub-Niggurath's sixteen in round 92, so 100 now); 'Umr at-Tawil's and Nyarlathotep's new frames are in place on `creatures-3.png` (which grew a shelf at its foot for 'Umr's right view, and a second for the Chaos's four added side-walk frames each way).
- `public/creatures/reviewed/source-index.json`: the active source registrations used to build this pack.

These files are copied into `dist/creatures/reviewed/` by the normal production build and served by the existing Electron package. No separate sprite folder needs to be installed into the game.

The seven allies have their own lossless atlas in `public/creatures/allies/` (`index.json`, `source-index.json`, `allies-0.png`: 272 unique 64×64 frames, 348 logical frames). It loads beside the 105-creature atlas and the two are joined in memory; the enemy pages are not repacked. Nodens, the Tritons, the Cats of Ulthar, Pickman and Nasht & Kaman-Thah walk on six frames. The Light-Being from Algol and the Yithian Archivist glide on four. Each has two idle, three attack and two hurt frames in front, left, back and right. A right view mirrors its left except the Yithian's, which is drawn. Each keeps the world height of the sprite it replaces.

## Animation behavior

Front, back, left and right are selected from the creature's facing relative to the camera, with a ten-degree margin to prevent flickering between views. A right view mirrors its source only when the registration explicitly requests it. The normal Innsmouth Hybrid has its two physical side folders labeled in reverse; its complete idle/walk view registrations are routed to the correct facing, with their pixels intact. Its eldritch variant retains its authored registration. Each accepted walk uses its actual two, four, six or eight frames over one second; direction changes retain the same cycle phase. Idles use their full registered frame count over 1.25 seconds. Combat poses follow the existing windup, strike, recovery and reaction timing. Where the library supplies combat art only for the front, that authored action is used; the nine bosses (the colossi, Yog-Sothoth, the Dunwich Horror) also have their attack and hurt drawn from their left and from their back, so a boss that swings while it is circled is drawn from the side it is seen, and its right is its left mirrored once (`reviewedClips`).

All frames of a creature keep one canvas scale and ground anchor taken from its front idle. Boss height follows the existing world scale; Cthulhu retains its 128×160 aspect. Reviewed PNG alpha is transparency, rather than the old procedural eye-glow marker. Procedural row distortion, void replacement and automatic recoloring do not modify these sprites, except on a hallucination (and a boss's decoy), which slips and glitches as it always has: that glitch is the mind's tell that it was never there. World lighting, fog, damage flashes and visibility still apply.

## Inspecting the artwork

- `?bestiary` displays all 112 authored forms (105 reviewed enemies and seven allies), cycling every registered direction and action. Each card identifies the current view, action and frame; clicking it spawns that exact form in the arena.
- `?spawn=deep_one&debug` starts a creature test with read-only animation diagnostics.
- `?spawn=deep_one&variant=eldritch&debug` tests a variant.
- `?spawn=cthulhu&debug` tests the tall boss canvas.

Gameplay definitions, AI, collisions, attack hit windows and saves are unchanged by the artwork import.

## In-game audit (2026-10-13)

All 105 forms were put in the arena (`?spawn=<id>[&variant=…]&debug`) with the world held still and read through `render_game_to_text()`: four idle views (front, left, back, right), a move, the first attack, a stagger and the death, each checked for the view, state, frame, source file and flip it should show; the nine bosses were also seen whole from a distance, and the rest from beside the investigator. Every form reports what it should, none is left undrawn for want of a clip, every one stands on the ground at a size its body fits (art height against the combat capsule: 0.6–1.7×; Martin's Beach Horror and the Yekubian stand taller than their capsule, Nug and the Formless Spawn shorter), and none is cropped. What looks missing is the game hiding it: the Dunwich Horror until revealed, the ambushers and burrower (Beast in the Cave, Formless Spawn, Nameless City Reptile, Dhole) until the investigator is near, Being from Beyond on its hidden layer, and the invisible stalkers (Flying Polyp, the Unnamable, Being from Beyond) as a shimmer until they strike.

Mended: hallucinations and decoys had lost their glitch (the integration set every form's weirdness to 0), so they looked exactly like real creatures.

Mended afterwards, on the author's asking (none of it redraws a frame):

- The outer gods (Azathoth, Shub-Niggurath, Yog-Sothoth) trail their two dithered echoes again, drawn from the same art at the lag, share and drift the 3D bodies had (`ECHOES`, `echoDrift` in `render/eldritch.ts`).
- Reviewed creatures and bosses keep their authored colours under the world's light. The added pale rims and shading floor have been removed at the author's request.
- The five lathed colossi (Cthulhu, Father Dagon, Mother Hydra, Ghatanothoa, the Colossus) are struck where their picture shows them: `tools/reviewed_look.py` measures the half-width of each boss form's front idle in the three bands its hurt zones are (legs, torso, head; `ZONES` in `data/bossTuning.ts`) as the 75th percentile of its rows, into `REVIEWED_BODY`; `toCombatant` gives it to a lathed body as `assembly.painted`, and `zoneShapes` makes each band at least that wide (never narrower than before). A mound (Azathoth: a low mass under motes that fill the picture; Shub-Niggurath's was one too, until round 92 drew her a tall figure that its zones still enclose) and a body of spheres (Yog-Sothoth: `hit`) keep their own.
- Yog-Sothoth is struck where its spheres are painted: its recipe carries seven spheres fitted to the front art (`hit`), ringed about the body so they stand as seen from any side, none past a blade's reach of its body, the lowest on the ground (`pictured` in `data/assemblyShape.ts`; `tests/hurtZones.test.ts` ties them to the art's own scale).
- Eyes glow in the dark as the investigator nears, and look out of a mist, for fourteen forms whose eyes were found and looked at (`REVIEWED_EYES`, same tool; round 83 added the Hound, the Haunter of the Dark, the serpent man and the Outsider; a mark that is a tooth, a gem or a dim amber was not taken). The same tool measures the exact colour of seventeen forms' lit marks (`REVIEWED_GLINTS`) and each form's own matter (`REVIEWED_MATTER`), which the per-form recolour reads. Front view only: the art holds them nowhere else.

Left as they are, for the author:

- Attack and hurt art exist only from the front, for all 105 forms: seen from the side or behind, a creature that strikes or is struck shows its front. Side and back art for those two states would end it.
- The seven colossi whose weak head comes down after each blow of their own no longer showed it: the stoop hint and the head zone worked, but the picture stood straight. (Mended since: see "The stoop" below.)
- The colossi are painted 1.3 to 1.65 times wider than their combat radius (Shub-Niggurath's new body, since round 92, is as wide as her mound: 0.99), so the investigator can stand inside the painted body of all but her; widening the capsules is a balance change, and was not made.
- Eyes of the forms not in `REVIEWED_EYES` (the Shoggoths' green spots, the rat swarm's and Wamp's amber, and any seen from the side) are not given a glow.

## The stoop

Seven colossi (the Colossus, Cthulhu, Father Dagon, Mother Hydra, Ghatanothoa, Azathoth, Shub-Niggurath) have a drawn pose for the moment their head comes down: two front frames, `stoop_0` (the bow) and `stoop_1` (the hold), on the canvas, scale and ground line of their front idle, in the palette of that idle (the author's `Seventy-Steps-Colossi-Front-Bow-Hold-oct13` delivery). A third frame, `stoop_mid` (the author's `Seventy-Steps-Colossi-Stoop-Mid` delivery), is the pose halfway between the idle and the bow, on the same canvas and ground line and no lower than the bow nor taller than the idle (a mound's height barely changes, so its halfway shape differs instead). While `stooped()` (`systems/hurt.ts`: recovering from the creature's own blow and a little after, or reeling from a stagger or a parry) is true, the billboard comes down through that pose for `FEEDBACK.midSeconds`, shows the bow for `FEEDBACK.bowSeconds`, then holds the last frame until the stoop ends; it then stands up through the halfway pose for `FEEDBACK.riseSeconds` and goes back to its idle (without the halfway frame, as it was: the bow at once, the idle at once); the weak-head hit zone is the same, so what is struck is what is shown. The art is front only, so from the side or behind the front pose is used, as for attack and hurt. The outer gods' echoes bow a moment behind. `?bestiary` cycles it with the other actions.

## Later deliveries

The frames are added to the pack by `python3 tools/add_reviewed_frames.py [--dry] <delivery folder>` (Pillow): the folder's one `*MANIFEST.json` names, for a front-only action (the stoop, `stoop_mid`) or by view (the bosses' left and back attack and hurt), each frame's path, hash and bounds. It checks each frame's bytes against the manifest, that the canvas and the idle (`idleSha256`) are those of its view in the pack, and that its ground line stands within two pixels of the idle's (exactly, for the stoop's; four for Yog-Sothoth, whose base drifts); refuses a view that already has the action; puts the frames on a page of their own after the pack's; registers them in `index.json` and `source-index.json`; and the integrity test holds them to the same account as the library (2,850 frames, 1,096 scopes). `--dry` checks everything and writes nothing. A creature the author drew again whole ('Umr at-Tawil: no manifest, the library's tree of PNGs) goes in by `python3 tools/replace_reviewed_sprite.py [--dry] <key> [<key> ...] <folder>` (or `--all <folder>`: every key of the folder's `MANIFEST.json`): each frame is pasted over the old one's rectangle on its page, a view he had by mirroring (his right) and the delivery draws on its own gets its frames on a new shelf at the foot of the page and no longer mirrors, and the hashes, bounds and both indexes follow. It also takes a scope drawn with more frames than it had (the Crawling Chaos's side walks, two made six: the old frames' places kept, the rest on the shelf, after them), a variant's key (`nyarlathotep#eldritch`: its frames are `sprites/nyarlathotep/eldritch/64x64`), and a view the pack mirrors that the delivery sends as files (Nyarlathotep's right, when they are its left turned round pixel for pixel: it stays a mirror, and nothing is added); a view it draws instead is whole, so its other actions that came as the source turned round are put on the shelf with it, and an action the delivery leaves out, or a drawn view shorter than the mirrored one was, is refused; a scope that grows grows in every view that mirrors it. A scope may also shrink (the frames nothing uses are cleared and taken out of the index, those after them move up), and a creature the manifest registers by its central body (a flier, or Yog-Sothoth, whose picture floats) is not held to the ground line. Attack and hurt are front art only, here and in every later delivery: a left, right or back attack or hurt in a folder is never packed, and the drawing it replaces has its own dropped with it (Yog-Sothoth's left and back, which no longer match the new body), so seen from a side the creature strikes or is struck as its front. The painted body measured from each boss's idle for its hurt zones, and the look of each form's edge, are regenerated with `python3 tools/reviewed_look.py`. `tools/pack_reviewed_assets.py` rebuilds the pack from the library alone, so after a repack run the tools again on each delivery in the order they came (or add them to the library's registrations first).

### The sixteen of round 56 (the 'Repairs v4' delivery)

Sixteen creatures drawn again whole, in 64 by 64 frames (Yog-Sothoth's in 128): Yig, the Haunter of the Dark, the High Priest's true form, Rhan-Tegoth, the Dhole, Yog-Sothoth, the Man of Leng, the Serpent Man, the Flying Polyp, the Ym-bhi, the Daemon Pipers, the Other Gods, Lilith, the Being of Ib, the Shunned House's entity and the Great Ones. Each has idle and walk in all four views, its right the left turned round, and attack and hurt from the front alone (`tests/redrawnCreatures.test.ts` holds the counts, the delivery's own hashes, and that no side or back attack or hurt is in the pack for them). They were put in with `replace_reviewed_sprite.py --all`; **keep the delivery folder**, for `pack_reviewed_assets.py` knows nothing of it and a repack loses these (run the tools again on each delivery, in order). `reviewed_look.py` was run again: the only change is Yog-Sothoth's painted body.

Their hurtboxes are fitted to the new bodies. A creature's capsule comes from its silhouette's fractions of its `sprite.scale`; the entry sets `sprite.body: [height, radius]` where its art needs different dimensions. Width is measured as the 85th percentile of painted pixels' distance from the billboard's middle over front, side and back idles. Yig, the Dhole and the Shunned House also use the tallest painted idle height across every view: 4.5 m, 3.9375 m and 1.6875 m, with their established radii of 1.5 m, 1.5 m and 0.84 m. The capsule moves what is struck; the height blows are scaled by and shots are aimed from (`reachOf`, carried as `reach`) stays the silhouette's, so their fights are as balanced (a taller aim point made a Dhole summoned by Yog-Sothoth take that fight past the colossus limit). A variant that draws another silhouette drops the base's `body`. The Shunned House's radius stays wider than its 0.64 m painted body to keep its fight above the balance table's six-second floor. Yog-Sothoth's seven `assembly.hit` spheres follow its front idle. `tools/reviewed_look.py` measures the physical sphere cluster's foot separately from detached sparks (`REVIEWED_GROUND`, row 111 of its 128 px canvas), and `reviewedSize` uses that fixed ground registration in every view and pose. This aligns the artwork with its grounded hit spheres at the existing canvas scale and keeps the lowest sphere within melee reach. Tests compare rendered sphere heights and radii with their native pixel coordinates and check all 23 poses against the physical body, while the table of body adjustments is in docs/DECISIONS.md.

### Nug, Yeb and Shub-Niggurath (round 92, the 'Lore Overhauls' delivery)

The author's `Seventy-Steps-Nug-Yeb-Shub-Lore-Overhauls-Full` zip holds 93 PNGs of three new designs made from the lore alone (its `LORE.md` says what Lovecraft wrote and what the art adds): Nug, a pale folded membrane with a deep red mouth and gripping limbs; Yeb, a dark ropy form on long legs; Shub-Niggurath, a towering mass of peat and charcoal folds with a crown of curling tendrils, thick roots and three mouths, in place of the dark mound under its motes. Nug and Yeb are 64 by 64, Shub-Niggurath 128 by 128, each with idle (2 frames) and walk (4) in all four views, its right the left turned round pixel for pixel, and attack and hurt from the front only (Nug 3 and 2, Yeb 3 and 2, Shub-Niggurath 4 and 4); hers also holds the bow and hold of the stoop (2 frames) and the pose halfway down to it, which is her attack_2 drawing again (the delivery's own choice, `art/shub_niggurath/STOOP-MID-DECISION.json`). They were put in by `python3 tools/replace_reviewed_sprite.py --all <delivery folder>`, which for this delivery learnt to take a colossus's stoop frames, pasting each over the old one in place (the count must be the old count; the frame must stand on the very ground line of the delivery's front idle and be no taller than it). The result: 23 frames of Nug (17 in place, 6 on a shelf at the foot of page 3), 23 of Yeb (17 in place, 4 in the places its own right view left, 2 on the shelf), 26 of Shub-Niggurath (20 in place, 4 in the places her own right left, 2 on the shelf) and her three stoop frames in place; Yeb's and Shub-Niggurath's own right views are gone (their rights are their lefts turned round now), and so are **Shub-Niggurath's left and back attack and hurt** (16 frames): they were drawn for the old mound, and by the standing rule (attack and hurt are front art only) she strikes and is struck from the side as her front, as Yog-Sothoth does. The pack holds 2,850 frames from 2,856 and 1,096 scopes from 1,100. **Keep the author's zip** (`/mnt/project-files/sprites/Seventy-Steps-Nug-Yeb-Shub-Lore-Overhauls-Full.zip` in the project's files), for `pack_reviewed_assets.py` knows nothing of these three, and run the tool on a fresh extraction of it: the project's file store signs every PNG it holds with a C2PA `caBX` chunk, so the folder extracted beside the zip (`lore-overhauls-oct27/`) has other bytes than the manifest's (the same pixels), and a rerun from it would register other `sourceSha256`s and fail `tests/loreOverhauls.test.ts`, which pins the manifest's.

`reviewed_look.py` was run again: the matter of the three (what a blow throws off them and what they crumble to) is now their browns and greys, Shub-Niggurath's painted body (`[0.38, 0.372, 0.297]`, which her mound's zones are never narrower than, and are not: `tests/loreOverhauls.test.ts`), and **Yeb is no longer in `GLINTED`**: its old art had one pale eye, kept alight in the dark, and the new Yeb has none; the one colour the finder found in its new frames is a highlight scattered along its limbs, which would have sparkled all over it at night. ## Rebuilding and checking

Run `python3 tools/pack_reviewed_assets.py --source /path/to/accepted/library --output public/creatures/reviewed` with Pillow available. The source folder must contain its current `CURRENT-GAME-ANIMATIONS.json` and every referenced PNG. The packer rejects changed source hashes and verifies every RGBA crop after packing.

`npm run check:all` includes checks for all 105 registry keys, all 1,096 animation scopes (1,050 of the library, the stoop and its halfway pose of seven colossi, and the seven other bosses' left and back attack and hurt), every packed frame's RGBA hash and bounds, native canvases, mirroring, variable animation timing and Electron asset serving. `npm run build` includes the artwork in the production game.
