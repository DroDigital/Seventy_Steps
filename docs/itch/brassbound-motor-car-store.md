# Seventy Steps — The Brassbound Motor Car

Take to the roads in a circa-1914 touring motor car, with black enamel, brass lamps, spoked wheels and a weathered folding canopy.

**Optional cosmetic DLC for [Seventy Steps](https://digitaldro.itch.io/seventy-steps). Requires the updated itch.io desktop game for Windows, macOS or Linux with mount unlock-file support. This is not a standalone game.**

The Brassbound Motor Car has the same speed and handling as the Chariot included in the base game: **10 m/s cruising and 17 m/s galloping**. It grants no gameplay advantage. Complete *The Borrowed Reins* by speaking with Peaslee and finding the Sleeper’s Sign before using any mount.

**US $0.99 minimum**, plus any applicable tax. Complete your purchase through itch.io checkout and choose PayPal to pay with PayPal.

## Install your appearance

1. After payment, download `brassbound-motor-car.seventysteps-dlc` from your itch.io purchase page.
2. Open the updated desktop game and visit an Elder Sign.
3. Open **Mounts**, choose **Import mount unlock file**, and select the downloaded file.
4. Select **The Brassbound Motor Car**. If needed, choose **Refresh DLC ownership**.

Keep the file or your purchase link so you can install it again. Normal game updates preserve your saves and imported appearance. This purchase unlocks the Brassbound Motor Car only and does not include a Steam DLC key.
