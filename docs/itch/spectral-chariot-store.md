# Seventy Steps — The Spectral Chariot

Travel through the Dreamlands in a black mourning carriage drawn by two skeletal horses, with violet fire along their bones.

**Optional cosmetic DLC for [Seventy Steps](https://digitaldro.itch.io/seventy-steps). Requires the updated itch.io desktop game for Windows, macOS or Linux with mount unlock-file support. This is not a standalone game.**

The Spectral Chariot has the same speed and handling as the Chariot included in the base game: **10 m/s cruising and 17 m/s galloping**. It grants no gameplay advantage. Complete *The Borrowed Reins* by speaking with Peaslee and finding the Sleeper’s Sign before using any mount.

**US $0.99 minimum**, plus any applicable tax. Complete your purchase through itch.io checkout and choose PayPal to pay with PayPal.

## Install your appearance

1. After payment on itch.io, choose **Claim** on the purchase page, then **Claim & download unlock** on the redemption page to save your `.seventysteps-dlc` file.
2. Open the updated desktop game and visit an Elder Sign.
3. Open **Mounts**, choose **Import mount unlock file**, and select the downloaded file.
4. Select **The Spectral Chariot**. If needed, choose **Refresh DLC ownership**.

Keep the downloaded file as a backup. Each purchase issues one unlock; you can retry an interrupted download in the same browser session. If your purchase is already claimed elsewhere, check your downloads or contact the publisher for recovery. Normal game updates preserve your saves and imported appearance. This purchase unlocks the Spectral Chariot only and does not include a Steam DLC key.
