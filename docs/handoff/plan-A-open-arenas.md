# Plan A: the 19 open-world arenas

Merged draft (before the skeptical critic's review, which was still running). Generated by a planning workflow; read as a proposal, not a decision.

- **overview**: One plan, four structure families plus two shared layers. Every one of the 19 arenas stays enclosed (one gate on an axis, sealed until found:<boss>, lethal or solid everywhere else) but each is a different KIND of place drawn from its own story. Kind per horror. OUTLINE (a plan drawn as a polygon or curve and filled with prefab building ranges, 8): Medusa = the burned plantation house Riverside (U of mansion and wings); Hutchinson = Curwen's Pawtuxet keep (donjon, lean-to lab, stack-chimney farmhouse, oaken door); Nug = a blank windowless basalt cella; Bokrug = the burst bronze gates of Sarnath fronting Ib's monolith wall; Martin's Beach = a bathing esplanade crescent closed by the holding hulk; Yig = a serpent effigy whose jaws are the gate; Daemon Pipers = a stone pipe organ; Yog-Sothoth = a Cyclopean abnormal-masonry bay holding the Ultimate Gate's living veil. GROUND (explicit-level pads plus colliders, 5): Colour = a dry reservoir basin; Black Man = the white-stone island in a black river; Colossus = the buried hypostyle under a dune; Other Gods = the carved summit of Hatheg-Kla; Cthulhu = a drowned forecourt: platform, lagoon, quay and the Great Door. WALLGRAPH (seeded solvable wall graphs, 3): Shub = an annular living thicket; Orne = a cemetery of tomb lots; Zkauba = the metal labyrinth of Yaddith. ASSEMBLY (figures and masses on a ring, tiers or a curve, 3): Hastur = the amphitheatre of Hali; Azathoth = the ring of blind dancers; Ancient Ones = the council on an impossible curve of hexagonal prisms. Merge choices: Colour took set B's basin over set A's farmyard; Black Man set B's island over A's garret (a Witch House dungeon already exists); Shub B's labyrinth over A's canopy hall; Orne B's cemetery grid over A's tomb-bank ring (a reskin); Hutchinson B's canon Pawtuxet over A's invented Rakus; Bokrug B's Sarnath gates over A's lagoon quay (one fewer water site); Colossus a merge (A's dune skirt, B's sunken court, impossible at this depth); Yig A's effigy over B's spiral (so Shub is the only concentric maze); Zkauba, Hastur, Azathoth, Ancient Ones, Cthulhu, Medusa, Martin's, Nug, Pipers, Yog mostly agree and are merged. Engine findings that corrected both concept sets (verified in the repo this pass): (1) the sea plane is -2 and falls.ts drowns ground below -1.6, so a walkable floor can never be dug below about -1.0: set B's 7-9 m sunken courts at Colour (y 4.15) and Colossus (y 3.29) are impossible and become raised rims and dunes round a floor left at land height; only annular moats go below -2.5 on purpose. (2) A heightfield slope is not a barrier because movement has no slope limit, so every enclosure still needs collider chords. (3) Splitting one arena mesh into sectors multiplies draw calls (materials x sectors), so cap at 3 sectors. (4) worldScene.ts:54 streams an arena by radius+12, not by its house, contrary to DECISIONS round 63; it must follow the plan's footprint. (5) arenaSets.test.ts:91 counts a baked model as used only if a set or a way mark stands it. (6) Hastur is a called boss that spawns 12 m ahead of the player wherever the third band falls, so no structure holds its first fight until the call is changed. Build order proves the riskiest engine pieces first: the seam (refactor, no visual change), then the structure-view host and polygon planner on Hutchinson, then explicit-level pads, lethal moats and place looks on the demo's Colour and Black Man, then the remaining outlines, mazes, ground and assemblies, with Cthulhu and the Hastur sim change last. Data and tests stay boss-keyed so discovery, courts, ways, map lead, hints and saves (ow.told keys) are untouched.
