# Plan B: the 27 dungeon and lair boss halls (and the bosses with neither)

Merged draft (before the skeptical critic's review, which was still running). Generated by a planning workflow; read as a proposal, not a decision.

- **overview**: One plan, three layers, no change to the playable box. (1) STRUCTURE: every one of the 27 boss halls becomes a different kind of place built AROUND the unchanged 48x48 m hall and its r23 fight circle: a render-only 'skin' that carries the hall's walls up past the 6 m line (steeple, stair-well, drum, funnel, barn nave, folded panels, relief course), a precinct on the land outside (island with causeway, château with moat, plinth hill with stone ring, dig-site maze, necropolis, monastery, street, twin houses, farmstead, canal wharf), and render-only 'crowns' above the roofline (spire, domes, star-cones, breached pyramid, gate rings, aerial mast) that make each place a landmark from afar. (2) MOTION: a new data-driven kinetic layer (hallViews, copied from the wayViews pattern: made when near, disposed when far, instanced above ~30 copies) with a small vocabulary (spin, orbit, bob, sway, pendulum, slide, hinge, pulse, chase, flow, face, flock, droop) driven by STAGES that follow the boss fight (Fight phase, signature counters, flood, lamps, control_swap, time_skip), plus hall air (the 'no air in the halls' rule of round 62 is reversed), exterior life (dungeonLife: steam, vents, blackout, bell, streamers, silhouettes), hall water and growth/cloth/void shaders. (3) DIMENSIONS: five halls (Keziah, Nyarlathotep, Umr, Hypnos, Zann) swell into a pocket look ('pocket:<id>' key passed to look/sky/mist/skyline; no new REGIONS) when a fight phase turns, using the Kadath gate (real portal) only where it already exists. Design rule for all 27: the fight circle (r23) is inscribed in the 48 m square, so free architecture lives in the four corner spandrels (about 11 m on the diagonal), overhead above 6 m (skin), and in the 1 m walls (sliding panels); set pieces still keep to the 0.78-1.0 ring and 5 m cap of hallSets.test. Two concept sets were merged: the HallSpec/stages/precinct/pads/pocket-key foundation, shaft skins, tide works and necropolis grounds come from the second set; the corner-spandrel rule, GroundFx.steady marks, per-boss kind choices (gilded Outsider ballroom, silk pavilion, Kadath citadel with seal-counting domes, mirrored Ephraim study, Rhan-Tegoth wax museum) come from the first. Engine risk is front-loaded: kinetics on Dr Munoz and the Terrible Old Man, moat/pads/crowns on Charles le Sorcier (a spike proves the sea plane fills an inland ring first), tide on Dagon, shaft skin on the Haunter and the Voice, pocket key on Keziah; the seven demo halls (Keziah, Munoz, Charles, Outsider, Hound, Unnamable, Voice) are all finished by phase 6. Effort for one engineer who knows the repo: foundation across phases 0-2 about 5-6 weeks, each family 2-4 weeks after that, about 5-6 months solo for 31 places, parallelisable by family from phase 3.
