# Plan C critic review (skeptical engineer + Lovecraft scholar)

## infeasible
- CLOCK SHIFT rests on a clock that is not saved and already disagrees with itself. g.frame starts at 0 (systems/game.ts:109) and is not in SaveData, so every load restarts at CLOCK.start 0.3 ('deep'); a saved ow.clockShift would be added to a base that resets, so 'waning' after a reload depends on a past session. Render and sim clocks also diverge: main.ts:264-265 does `simTime += dt; if (!cinema.step(dt)) return;`, so simTime keeps running through every boss arrival/fall cutscene while g.frame (used by discovery.holds, minimap.ts:47, npcLife, wanderers) stops. The sky's hour and a vigil's hour therefore differ by the total cutscene time, and Yog and Nyarlathotep make that visible. paneLife.ts:36 also computes phase inside GLSL from a raw `t` uniform, so the plan's 'eight call sites' undercounts. Fix: keep the shift as a session-only Game field (or save the whole clock), define one clockSeconds(g, alpha) and route render and sim through it. Note the first gloaming is 756 s after a load and the first waning 432 s.
- RIFT-LITE is costlier than 'M'. portalViews.sync() (portalViews.ts:138-146) only adds views; `views`/`byGate` have no delete path, so a door that stands only in the gloaming (Nyarlathotep) or beside the investigator (Yog's sphere) leaves its veil mesh forever unless teardown is added. build() resolves the destination through gatePlace(id).to (beyond(), line ~95), so a rift id falls back to the hub look. gateWalk.ts:41 and :82 iterate worldLayout().gates, so rifts.ts must re-implement step-into detection, GateEntering/Travelled, journeys.go and the arrival. passGate, leadsTo and gateNear all look up the twin by id. Treat the P8 core tier and 'cinema without a door' as the baseline and the rift as an add-on, not the other way round.
- THE EXHIBITION cannot be authored in the cinema's data schema. data/cutscenes.ts Scene is camera shots plus beats (caption, title, fade, sound, set, burst, shake, rise, kneel, voice). BurstKind is ash, motes, embers, stars, frost, brine, spores, smoke, runes, notes, eyes, and Subject is target, player or sign. There are no actors, crowd, instruments, screen or stair. 'Seated crowd rising and filing down a stair, instruments throwing sparks, a screen of the investigator's shadows' is a new scene-set system (render-only npcFigures crowd, a screen mesh, a stair, choreography), so P14 is H, not M-H. Cheaper and truer to the poem: a scene at the sign with notes/eyes/runes bursts, tinted fades, captions lifted from the prose poem, and voice beats. Any spoken line needs recordings or tests/voiceCoverage fails npm run check.
- TIME-STOP touches far more than the plan lists. main.ts render() passes `time` to about 25 updaters (views, lights, creatures, hidden, fights, signs, portals, bossFx, particles, fxController, life, updateWorldUniforms, updatePostUniforms and more). The plan names only look, sky, fog, particles and weather. If creatures/lights keep real time, 'dust hangs' beside foes that still animate; if they freeze, sprites slide while the sim moves them. It is picture-only: g.frame, foes and projectiles keep running, so 'the world holds still' is an illusion and must be worded that way. P1 builds this plumbing for Azathoth, who does not need it; defer worldTime to P5, the only consumer.
- HANDBILLS are not persistent as described. A runtime spawnTome is not recreated on load (checkpoints.ts:48 restores only layout tomes), so a bill dropped where a boss fell vanishes on reload. Bills in DOCUMENTS also break the 'The Whole Library' achievement (achievements.ts:30 counts Object.keys(DOCUMENTS)): about 30 boss-fall bills are unreachable for any save that killed a boss before the second seal or before P12. Use one handbill document read from many tomes, or exclude them from the count. Save the bill spots or hand the bill to the journal, and grant bills retroactively from `slain` for existing saves.
- NG+ DROPS the new state. systems/cycles.ts:49-50, 76 and 90-91 carry only hints and found:<id> for ids in DISCOVERIES. Umr's found, Nyarlathotep's called and found, ord:*, lead:* and rift:* are not carried. A second journey would repeat the Guide and the whole Exhibition, and the 'chaos' achievement (slay Nyarlathotep) would be gated behind it again. Extend the carry list and decide which of these a second journey keeps.
- ANCIENT ONES: Layer.minMind AND minFound cannot do what the plan says. layerShown ANDs every condition (hiddenLayer.ts:22-28), so a gate with both hides again when the mind drops after found, contradicting 'persists whatever the mind does'. Use 'minMind ignored once found' (OR semantics) or put minMind only on the lattice and decor pieces. The stated reason ('not sealed, so it uses minMind') is also wrong: `sealed` only picks the seal's look, 'sealed' or 'wall' (arenaHouse.ts:77). Every house gate already carries minFound.
- STRANGE 'OFF' has no completion path. The plan switches every rule off when strange='off' but does not say how the discovery finishes. Without a defined off-form for each Ordeal (hush, follow, avert, sit, pass), 'off' soft-locks Azathoth, Pipers, Other Gods, Ancient Ones and Yog. Give every ordeal an assist form and an off form that complete by plain geometry or time, and test all three modes in discovery.test.ts.
- ORDEAL ORDER DEADLOCK. discoverySystem plays steps strictly in order (the `turn` flag), and every page lies at the gate end of the court (leaf d=7 to 9 in discoveries*.ts). Hush (carried toward the gate) and the Pipers' follow (ends at the Organ's gate) finish at the same end, so the ordeal either completes before the page can be read or is ignored as 'not its turn'. The player then reads at the gate and walks 72 m back out to be carried again. Let ordeal progress count regardless of order, or move the leaves to the court's outer end (d near FOUND.court 46).
- ORDEAL KEYS do not fully protect old saves. mark() must still write disc:<boss>:<i> for the ordeal's index (currentStep, the quest 'step' goals and lead.ts all read stepKey), and discoverySystem skips any step whose stepKey is already told. Reshaping Pipers from [read, rite, vigil] to [read, follow] means an old save's disc:daemon_pipers:1 (the rite) credits the ordeal unplayed. Yog [read, rite, offer] to [read, vigil, offer] has the same problem. ord:<boss>:<rule> does not prevent this. Give steps stable ids (with an index fallback only for unchanged lists), or version the keys.
- LIVING HOUSES are mostly silhouette. A wall piece is a 4.6 m prop (HOUSE.segment) with a 4-5 m thick collider box (arenaHouse.ts:35,46). Dancers 22-34 m tall and about 43 m wide each can only be solid inside that thin ribbon. Anything wider must be non-solid and outside the 18 m walkable band between the fight ring and the wall, so from inside the house they read as a skyline. That is acceptable but should be stated. The shared vertex term, four new wall shapes, the aPhase bake and the overlay module are three PRs, not part of P1.

## lore
- OTHER GODS has the wise man backwards. In Lovecraft's 'The Other Gods' Barzai is 'the Wise' (the one who feared nothing and is taken); Atal is the disciple who turns back and lives. The plan says 'Atal, wise in his fear', and the existing discovery title and note ('The Wise Man's Fear', 'The wise man must be afraid, and wait', discoveriesEarth.ts) invert it. Fix the attribution. The avert rule also has direct textual support the plan does not use. As I recall it, Barzai's last cry names the Other Gods as the guardians of the 'feeble' gods of earth and begs Atal to look away and not see (verify the exact wording before quoting). Use that line for the omen and the shut notice. The look-up tier's 'given back at the sign' is the game's mercy, not canon: in the story only Atal returns.
- SHUB'S YOUNG that 'creep only when unwatched' are the Weeping Angels (Doctor Who, 2007), not the mythos. Lovecraft gives the Thousand Young only as an epithet ('the Black Goat of the Woods with a Thousand Young'; Whisperer in Darkness, Dunwich rites). The 'Dark Young' tree-things are Derleth/Campbell/Lin Carter, and SPEC section 3E (line 195) says the game's Thousand Young are original design. Replace the unwatched-creep with something Lovecraft supplies: the wood's sound (a bleating chorus, birds gone), the Dunwich whippoorwills (the game already has them in worldLife `fauna`) falling silent or gathering, the all-mother's pulse. Keep the gaze rule for the Other Gods alone.
- DAEMON PIPERS: Lovecraft has no pipers and no organ. The Haunter of the Dark has Azathoth 'lulled by the thin monotonous piping of a daemoniac flute held in nameless paws'. The 'drums and flutes' sentence belongs to the Dream-Quest and the 1920 'Nyarlathotep' prose poem (do not cite the Witch House for it without checking). A pipe organ is a church image, and 'mindless attendants who play for the one who does not know it' is invention. Draw the wall as reed-flutes or bone pipes with paws, and say that 'lulled' is canon. Note the existing fight inverts it: the game's Azathoth slumbers when the piping ends, whereas Lovecraft's is lulled by it.
- YOG-SOTHOTH: 'when the stars are right' is the Cthulhu/Old Ones idiom (Call of Cthulhu), not Yog's. His own texts are the gate-and-key litany and 'knows where the Old Ones broke through of old' (Dunwich Horror), and the Dunwich rites run on the calendar (May Eve, Hallowe'en, Lammas). A waning-hour window is fine as game design but the lore line 'the Mythos' own door' should be reworded. 'Congeries of iridescent globes' is the Silver Key image. I could not confirm it in Charles Dexter Ward, so drop that citation. 'Past, present, future all one in Yog-Sothoth' (Dunwich) is the real support for the three passes.
- UMR AT-TAWIL: the veil hiding 'the face of the sleeper' is the plan's invention. In Through the Gates of the Silver Key Umr at-Tawil is a separate Ancient One, the veiled guide who shows Carter that he is many; it is Carter's multiplicity, not Umr's face. The plain wooden door and the dreamer's childhood belong to the earlier 'The Silver Key' (1926), not Through the Gates (1933). Both are fine as game readings, but label them so and do not present them as quoted. The invented reveal also duplicates the Ancient Ones' pale double (see alike).
- AZATHOTH: 'blind idiot god' and the encircling dancers are Haunter of the Dark; the 'daemon-sultan ... drums and flutes' sentence is the Dream-Quest (and the prose poem). The plan cites the Dream-Quest and the Witch House for the first. 'It hears' is the game's invention (Lovecraft's Azathoth is mindless and lulled), so 'standing still' as the way in is a gameplay reading, not canon; word the Field Guide and journal lines accordingly. The nine dancers are the game's own count.
- NYARLATHOTEP: the poem's show is attended in a hall and ends with the crowd walking down endless stairs under a failing moon. The plan's door-at-an-Elder-Sign inverts the Elder Sign, which is mostly a Derleth device; that is fine as an in-game inversion. The 'tall man in a good coat' and 'key on his watch chain' are the game's own lines (cutscenes.ts:162, bossCards.ts:49, npcs.ts:70) and already bind him to the Silver Key. Do not credit them to the poem.

## alike
- PIPERS and UMR are the same discovery. Both use the Leash: fixed stops, a thing one stop ahead, advance within about 9-10 m, hold while hunted, lost beyond about 40 m, progress saved as lead:<id>:<k>. Shub's 180 s safety net is a third use ('file'). Only the skin differs (a sound versus a veiled figure). Break the pair. Pipers: use what the lore asks for, which is the Other Gods dancing 'slowly, awkwardly, absurdly' to the piping. Cross the court by stepping or rolling on the flute's irregular accents, reusing the plan's own beat clock with a visual pulse as the second channel. Umr: the door is 'the dreamer's own', so make it appear when the investigator rests (sleeps) at the Beyond's Elder Sign with the page read. That uses an existing action and no new system, and echoes the intro's bench.
- FIVE OF EIGHT ARE 'HOLD STILL FOR N SECONDS'. Azathoth hush (stand still while carried), Other Gods avert (stand at the gate and look away 2.5 s, after a 30 s vigil), Ancient Ones sit (kneel and stay lucid 30 s), Yog (wait for the hour, then a 24 s vigil), Shub (wait for the exhale after a 28 s vigil). Three of the five proposed Ordeal rules are the existing vigil with a posture condition. The player's input is refraining in most of the Outer Gods. Make at least two of them active inversions (Pipers dancing, Shub losing the way, Nyarlathotep attending, Yog walking into the sphere) so the set is not one verb.
- UMR and ANCIENT ONES end on the same twist: the investigator's own body or double in the room (the sleeper under the veil, the pale double in the seventh seat). Both are Silver Key, both in the Beyond, both nearly the same reveal twice. Keep it for one. Suggest keeping the sleeper for Umr and leaving the Ancient Ones' seventh place empty (the lore is that it is left out), with the fight's last beat being a look at the gap.
- YOG and ANCIENT ONES are both time manipulations in the Beyond (clock shift versus stopped time) and both lean on hexagons. Yog's court already has hex inlays and hexpeds, so Place Skin 'threshold' (cycling looks) and 'pale_hexagons' (frozen) are two halves of one idea. Give Yog the spheres and sky, and the Ancient Ones the prisms and the stillness, with no shared hexagon vocabulary.
- MAP LIES recur in three plans (Azathoth hidden and moved, Yog 'everywhere', Nyarlathotep 'late'), and Azathoth and Nyarlathotep both lie the lead pointer. The game also already lies to a failing mind (ui/mapWrong.ts, round 26: slip and falseStar), and the Beyond drains the mind, so a deliberate place-based lie is lost in that noise. Pick one god for the lying map, and give the other a different strangeness.
- THE CAMERA AS INPUT is used twice (Other Gods avert, Shub's Young). The existing Gorgon discovery (discoveriesEarth.ts medusa_gorgon: 'to one who has not looked up', 'keep your eyes on it') is a third gaze flavour in text only. Keep the Gaze system for the Other Gods alone.
- ACOUSTICALLY, Azathoth and the Pipers are identical: 'a thin piping at the left ear' inside about 140 m of the Court (a lying gain curve) and inside about 120 m of the Organ (honest). The Elder Sign beyond_court (372,248) is 100 m from the lair (472,248), so the ending sign sits inside the Pipers' range, and the Azathoth skin's edge (144 m) is at the sign. One sound means opposite things in overlapping zones. Give the Court a drum and a different timbre or side, and keep the thin flute for the Organ alone.

## unfair
- AZATHOTH'S DRIFT PUNISHES THE RULE IT TEACHES. The Court's own way already has blight at d=84(0), 70(5), 56(-5), 42(0), 28(5), r 5.2, rate 3.2 mind/s, and vents at d=76, 60, 44, side +/-3.8 (burst 3 m, damage 28, warn 60; arenaWaysBeyond.ts:76-79). A body carried down the centreline from d=72 to 14 spends about 19 m in blight (10.4 m at (42,0) plus three 2.9 m chords). At 1.7 m/s that is about 11 s, so about 35 mind lost for doing exactly what the game asks. A roll to dodge a vent is noise 20, which cancels forward progress. Move or remove the centreline blight, or have the carry follow a clean line. Also: blight (84,0) sits about 6 m from the Elder Sign and the stretch is about 34 s of forced standing in the Beyond's drain.
- THE ROAD DOES NOT TEACH THE FIGHT. noise() decides nothing alone: AZATHOTH.hear (walk 7, attack 16, dodge 20, sprint 28, shot 45 m) is compared with the distance to the god (`gap > noise(g)` in the signature). In the 44 m arena, walking is safe beyond 7 m and sprinting beyond 28 m. The road's 'any noise cancels forward' is absolute. A player who learns the road creeps needlessly in the fight, and a player who knows the fight finds the road arbitrary. Mirror the comparator (heard from the Court: the nearer the gate, the quieter the body must be), or stop claiming it teaches the fight.
- AZATHOTH'S FINDING stacks four lies (map hidden and moved, a decoy ring, a sound with a wrong gain curve, a sky disc at the wrong elevation) against subtle honest cues. The lead pointer and beacons are also off. How does a player find the Court after Yog falls without the Field Guide, which is not shown on screen? Keep at least one honest long-range cue (the skyline dancers or a beacon) and make the lie finite and legible. Do not hide beyond_court for saves that already found it or slew Azathoth: it is the ending sign (systems/endings.ts COURT_SIGN offers 'seal' and 'herald' there).
- ANCIENT ONES: the time dome eases timeScale to 1 'in 1.5 s' at engage, but BOSS.wake is 45 frames (0.75 s). The first windup can therefore animate on the slowed render clock while damage lands on the sim clock, which makes the telegraph late or slow, so the first blow is unfair. The ease must finish before the first windup, or bossFx/decals/telegraphs must always read real time.
- SKIN VERSUS FIGHT CONTRADICTION in the Azathoth text: 'In the fight the ripple runs at the song bar's speed and each blast's spot swells', but the shared spec says Place Skin eases to nothing at engage. Decide. The windup swell and the 7 m no-displacement rule are good readability. A ripple while the player must creep is a comfort risk for the exact mechanic that needs steady reading.
- THE HUNTED RULE hides invisible hunters. Every Strange rule turns off when any brain is aware of the investigator, but the Beyond's Being from Beyond is visible only when Unmoored (SPEC line 195). A rule dropping out because of an unseen hunter looks like a bug. Use 'a visible foe within N m' or 'damaged in the last 10 s'.
- OTHER GODS avert: the shut notice 'THE STONES DO NOT PART BEFORE A FACE' reads as the character's face, but the test is the camera (the boom recentres behind a walking body, so it is a standing, camera-orbit act within 7 m of the gate). The only confirmation (runes dim when faced) arrives after the act. Add a pre-feedback line when the player lingers at the gate (about 10 s) without averting (LOOK AWAY), keep the 8 s wait as the assist, and test gamepad right-stick yaw. The black boss in a cloud lid with fog at 40 m and lightning capped at 2/s is dark; keep the faint edge and the existing 'darkness' hook in mind.
- SHUB'S MAZE collides with the discovery's own acts. The maze is d=10..87, but the vigil is at spot(16,-5.6), the altar at spot(12,4.8) and the page at d=7. The first two lie inside the maze rect. The memorandum at the maze's end is the first act, so the player reads last what they should read first. Blanking the map in the maze also removes the fast-travel screen, which is the escape hatch. Keep the star list (travel) available there, start the maze at d>=24 or move the acts, and prove it in enclosedAudit.
- NYARLATHOTEP'S ORCHESTRA PIT: the game teaches that a pit is a fall (FALL, falls.ts: stepping past a ledge is a death). A sunken pit that is 'decoration only, walkable around' is a false signal either way. Draw it as a railed or covered pit with a collider, or leave it out of the fight hall.
- THE DOOR HUNT: bills 'name an hour and no place', the lead lies (the star on the region next over), and the gloaming is 14% of the night (about 151 s) with the clock reset at each load, so the first gloaming is about 12.6 min after load. 'Wait for the hour' at a sign helps, but the third bill must say the door stands beside the sign one rests at, or the player cannot infer where.
- YOG'S PASSES are internally inconsistent. After pass 1 the clock is no longer waning (+1/3 night), so 'a sphere settles within 80 s in the waning hour' cannot apply to passes 2 and 3. Three passes of exactly night/3 return the clock to where it began, so the payoff is two sky jumps and 18 mind, with no change to the world, but the shift moves NPC schedules, wanderers, windows and the HUD clock in between. Consider doing the past/present/future by the Place Skin's sky alone and leaving the global clock out of it.
- UMR'S LEGACY RULE defeats the Guide. An InsightChanged handler that tells found:umr_at_tawil at insight >= 3 means any player with the main quest's 2 insight and one tome sees the span rise on arrival and never needs the Guide. Limit the legacy rule to existing saves (for example a one-time carry-forward when beyond_threshold or beyond_first was already found), not a live rule.
- THE BEYOND TILE IS CROWDED, and the plan's separate skins and leashes collide. The Court's gate is at z about 330 and its sign at d about 78. The Pipers' court runs west along z=248 from x about 426, and the sign is at d about 54, so the two courts cross at the Elder Sign beyond_court (372,248). The Pipers' star vents (d=22..80, side +/-3) stand beside the sign. The lure's half-spiral (radius 58-120 around (472,248), +/-100 degrees of west) reaches points about 25-60 m from the Court's gate and its 140 m skin, where the drift and the skin overlap. Yog's court runs east along z about 408 and reaches Azathoth's wall (x about 312) near d about 135, with Azathoth's skin covering the last half of it. The tile also holds Zkauba's lair and house (440,120) and the Rue d'Auseil (zann_window_thing; 80,220). Add pairwise tests (skin overlaps, leash stops outside the drift strip and the skins, court crossings) and fix the order of precedence: the nearer skin wins.
