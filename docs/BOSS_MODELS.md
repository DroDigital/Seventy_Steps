# The boss places' models: source and use

Generated by `tools/models/boss/mapping.py` from the manifests (`tools/models/boss/manifest_*.py`) and the baked index (`src/data/models/bossIndex.ts`).
A model's id is what the planners in `src/world/places/` and the sets of the boss halls and rooms (`src/data/hallSets*.ts`, `src/data/roomSets.ts`) name; `size` is its baked box (x × y × z metres) before a planner scales it. Licences and the changes made: `src/data/models/CREDITS.md`.
