# Since launch: the Chariot, smoother combat, and a more vivid Dreamlands

Thank you for playing Seventy Steps. There have already been quite a few updates since launch on 15 October, and I haven’t been keeping a devlog alongside them. This first post catches up on what has changed.

## A Chariot for your journey

You can now unlock a **free Chariot** through *The Borrowed Reins*: speak with Peaslee, then find the Sleeper’s Sign. Once unlocked, summon or dismiss it with **B on keyboard or L3 on a controller** using the default controls. Hold dodge/sprint while moving to gallop.

The Chariot has a new draft horse, animated gaits, hoof effects, and riding sounds that follow its movement and the ground beneath it. Mount prompts and the Mounts menu are available in English, German, French and Spanish.

It is for travelling across the open world. Entering a dungeon or boss arena dismisses it, and it cannot be summoned inside either. The riding prompts also clear after a few seconds.

## More room to defend and counter

- Stamina recovers faster, blocking costs less, and sprinting drains less stamina. Stamina also recovers while you hold your guard.
- The investigator is harder to stagger, and enemies leave longer gaps between attacks. Gug and Bokrug have received targeted difficulty adjustments.
- Bosses put less constant pressure on sanity. Laudanum now restores **50 sanity** and protects it for **20 seconds**, giving you more time to concentrate on the fight.
- Spare revolver ammunition is now limited to **12 rounds**, in addition to the loaded cylinder. Keep an eye on your supplies.

## Creatures and places with more character

Allies including Nodens, the Cats of Ulthar, Pickman and the Tritons now have their own authored sprites. The Shantak has its own flight animation, and the Tritons’ walking poses have been corrected.

Boss attacks have individual colours, projectiles and ground markings: tides, cracks, spores, runes and other signs suited to the horror casting them. Their areas and timing remain the same.

Dungeon lighting has been adjusted, harsh floor reflections reduced, and ocean waves kept out of dungeon interiors. Weather stays outside, with thunder muffled underground. Distant landmarks also remain visible as you approach them.

## Notes, menus and fixes

- Field notes, letters and reports open on burnt paper, including when you revisit them through the journal. Rumours can be read as written notes.
- Relics have distinct symbols, and the Elder Sign menus are simpler to navigate.
- Higher resolution modes are an explicit choice in Settings, alongside the standard retro options.
- Controllers connected after startup are detected, and fixes address unwanted pauses during focus changes and controller checks.
- Cutscenes protect the investigator while they play. Paired boss deaths, first conversations with allies, and dungeon entrances have also received fixes.

**Watch your footing:** falling off the land, into the sea, or through pits and bridge gaps is fatal. Your Lost Echoes remain on the last safe ground where you can retrieve them.

## How to get these updates

Install Seventy Steps through the free [itch.io app](https://itch.io/app) for automatic updates. If you downloaded it through your browser, close the game, download the latest build for your operating system from the [game page](https://digitaldro.itch.io/seventy-steps), extract the complete download, and launch the new copy.

**Your existing save games are kept when updating.** Saves live separately from the installation folder, so you can continue in the same slot on the same computer and user account. You do not need to start a new journey.

Thank you for spending time in this strange world. If something goes wrong, please leave a comment with what happened and which operating system you’re using.
