# CLAUDE.md

Lovecraftian open-world soulslike in retro 3D. **Spec: `docs/SPEC.md`.** At session start read it and this
file, then do only the phase named in the message. Log judgment calls in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

## Commands
- `npm run dev`: title (it opens out of the dark with its theme on the first key or click, at once where the browser allows sound; New game opens on the intro), then the open world saved to localStorage (click captures the mouse; keys rebindable in Controls; E rests,
  talks and passes a boss's fog (a realm's gate has no key: walk into it, and nearing one says where it leads); R Reagent, T Laudanum, G oil, V reload; M map (click a lit ★ twice: travel; right-click or F, the pad's Y, sets a marker the minimap points to); Esc/Start: pause, map, journal, arms, settings with their tabs: display, sound, play, controls). `?fresh` new game, no title; `?demo` the demo (the university and Arkham, to Keziah Mason); `?arena` combat arena; `?bestiary` every roster entity (click to fight); `?spawn=<id>
  [&variant=eldritch|boss]`; `?look` look test (1–9, drag/wheel/O); `?perf` the frame meter (F3 toggles it anywhere; it also says what the input hears: keys, mouse, the pad); `?bench` the benchmark; `?trailer[&hold=n]` the picture alone, turning about eight places (footage for a trailer); `?debug` debug panel (sliders, H hides) + `window.game`/`world`/`audio`/`life`/`cinema`/`pipeline` (`grab()`: the low-res picture)/`sky`/`scene`/`camera`/`settings`. ## Rules (spec §0, condensed)
- Do only the requested phase; never scaffold later ones. Plan in ≤15 lines, then execute without asking.
- Ambiguity: pick the simplest option that fits the spec, log one line in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, keep going.
- No file dumps or large diffs in chat. End each phase with a ≤10-line summary and a git commit.
- Surgical edits. Files under 300 lines with one responsibility each.
- Verify with `npm run check` and read only the failing output. Never open `node_modules/` or `dist/`.
- No asset files: textures, sprites, meshes and audio are generated in code. Exceptions: the arenas' models (round 61, asked for: `src/data/models`, a few score CC0 pieces (fifty-one since round 72) of Kenney's kits baked by `tools/models/bake_models.py` into compact data, never loaded as files and credited in `src/data/models/CREDITS.md`; every other piece of an arena's set is built in code; round 76, asked for: the leaves of the arenas' gates, `src/data/models/gate*.ts`, cut from CC0 models of Poly Haven and Kenney and, for Yig's head, the Court's ring and the Threshold's temple front, three CC BY 4.0 models of Sketchfab, credited with their changes), the title theme (`public/music`), the realms' background music (`public/music/realms`: fifteen tracks made with Suno, `public/music/CREDITS.md`), the boss themes (`public/music/bosses`: forty-eight themes made with Suno, untouched MP3s, also in `public/music/CREDITS.md`), the recorded sounds (`public/audio`: CC0 recordings cut and treated for the game, each credited in its `CREDITS.md`; playtest round 6) and the voices (`public/voice`: one recording of each line the people and the horrors say, made with ElevenLabs' Eleven v4 from `data/speech*.ts`, `docs/VOICES.md`) and the investigator (round 81, asked for: his figure and all his clips are modelled and keyed in Blender by code, `tools/player/`, and baked into `src/data/player` as numbers like the arenas' models, never loaded as files; no skeletal animation: a clip is a rotation for each rigid part on each frame) and the interface's two typefaces (round 74, asked for: `public/fonts`, IM Fell English for what is read (SIL OFL) and Special Elite for the intro's telegram (Apache 2.0), subset to what the game prints, with their licences and `public/fonts/CREDITS.md`; `ui/fonts` loads them before the game's module, `hudKit.SERIF` and `TYPEWRITER` name them with the system faces behind).
- Creatures are data, not classes (15–30 lines each); boss phase scripts are data too.
- Deps: `three` (+ `@types/three`), `typescript`, `vite`, `vitest`, `electron` (the desktop shell, playtest round 11), `gamepad-node` (the shell's controllers over SDL2, round 36), `steamworks.js` (the shell's Steam: achievements, overlay; round 47), `electron-builder` (packaging, round 47). - Simulation code (combat, stamina, sanity, AI, registry, streaming math) must not import Three.js.
- Out of scope: multiplayer, skeletal animation, imported models/textures (but for the arenas' baked CC0 models, above), mobile.

## Conventions
- Sim: fixed system order in `stepGame`; moves are 60 Hz frame windows `[from, to)`; yaw 0 faces +z, right = (−cos, sin).
- Colours are display sRGB end to end (`ColorManagement` off); every material is a `ShaderMaterial`.
- World materials share one uniform set (`worldUniforms`). FX flow: `FxState → computeFx() → update*Uniforms()`.
- Sanity FX are `[calm, mad]` ramps in `tuning.ts`, blended by stress = min(1 − sanity/100, fx cap); hooks listen to `SanityBandChanged`/`InsightChanged`.
- New creature: its id in `roster.ts` + an entry in its tier file (a `voice`, or its tier's); `npm run check` runs `validateRegistry()`.
- New realm: its look in `data/looks.ts` (its grade, accents, mist, sky, light and far colours); `npm run check` makes no two realms grade alike and keeps every colour that lights or fills the scene from reading as an anomaly hue.
