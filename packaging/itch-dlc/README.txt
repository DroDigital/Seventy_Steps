The Brassbound Motor Car
Cosmetic mount DLC for Seventy Steps

Requires the full Seventy Steps desktop game from itch.io, version 1.4.4
or later, on Windows, macOS or Linux.
https://digitaldro.itch.io/seventy-steps

1. Extract Brassbound-Motor-Car.zip to a folder you can find.
2. Start or continue your journey in Seventy Steps. Rest at any Elder
   Sign, open the Mounts tab and choose Import DLC.
3. Select brassbound-motor-car.seventysteps-dlc from the extracted files.
4. Complete The Borrowed Reins, then select The Brassbound Motor Car in
   the Mounts tab at an Elder Sign.

The game remembers the import for all journeys under your current OS
user profile. The car works offline. Keep a private backup of your
download in case you reinstall on another device or reset your profile.
